# Solavisetech Women in Data Cohort 3

## About This Journey

This repository documents my learning journey through the **Solavise Technology Women in Data Cohort 3** program.

Over the course of this three-month program, I aim to build a stronger foundation in data science, data analytics, and artificial intelligence while gaining practical experience through exercises, collaborative activities, and projects.

My goal is to go beyond learning theoretical concepts and develop the ability to apply what I learn to solve problems, analyze information, draw meaningful insights, and develop practical solutions.

## My Goals

By the end of this program, I aim to:

- **Build a strong foundation in data science** by understanding its fundamental concepts and how data can be used to answer questions and solve problems.
- **Develop practical data analysis skills** by learning how to explore, clean, interpret, and draw meaningful insights from data.
- **Strengthen my problem-solving abilities** by approaching challenges logically, asking relevant questions, and using evidence to support conclusions.
- **Develop an understanding of artificial intelligence** by exploring its fundamental concepts, applications, and potential to solve real-world problems.
- **Gain hands-on experience** by applying what I learn through practical exercises and individual or collaborative projects.
- **Improve my analytical and critical-thinking skills** by identifying patterns, interpreting findings, and drawing reasonable conclusions from data.
- **Develop my teamwork and communication skills** by collaborating with others, exchanging ideas, and contributing meaningfully to group projects.
- **Build confidence in presenting my work** by learning to explain my approach, communicate findings clearly, and reflect on the results of my efforts.
- **Prepare for continued learning** by establishing a foundation for further development in data science, machine learning, and AI engineering.

## What I Hope to Achieve

By the end of the program, I want to have more than completed a series of lessons. I want to understand the concepts I have studied and demonstrate that understanding through practical work.

My intended outcomes are:

1. A stronger understanding of data science, data analytics, and artificial intelligence fundamentals.
2. Practical experience applying data-related concepts to exercises and projects.
3. Completed projects that demonstrate my ability to approach problems and apply what I have learned.
4. Experience working collaboratively, sharing responsibilities, and contributing to a team's progress.
5. Greater confidence in analyzing data, interpreting results, and communicating insights.
6. A collection of documented work that reflects my progress and can serve as a starting point for my future portfolio.

## My Learning Approach

Throughout this journey, I want to focus on understanding concepts rather than simply memorizing information. I aim to practice consistently, ask questions when I encounter difficulties, learn from mistakes, and use feedback to improve.

I also recognize that developing technical skills takes time and that completing this program is only one step in a longer learning journey. I intend to approach the experience with curiosity, consistency, and a willingness to learn from both successes and challenges.

## Progress and Documentation

I will use this repository to document my progress throughout the program. As I learn and complete practical work, I will add relevant exercises, project documentation, analyses, and reflections.

This repository will serve as a record of what I have worked on, what I have learned, the challenges I have encountered, and how my understanding develops over time.

## Looking Beyond the Program

Participating in Women in Data Cohort 3 is an opportunity to strengthen my knowledge, gain practical experience, and explore the wider possibilities within the data field.

Beyond the program, I aim to continue developing my skills in data science, machine learning, and AI engineering. I hope to build on the foundation established during these three months and use it as a stepping stone toward more advanced learning and meaningful projects.

Ultimately, I want to become someone who can understand technical concepts, apply them to real problems, and continuously grow through practice and experience.
